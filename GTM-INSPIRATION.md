## Worked examples informing the method

- [Creator discovery](https://treg.to/workflows/discover-creators-in-a-niche) and [screening](https://treg.to/workflows/screen-instagram-creators-before-outreach): qualify relevance using recent evidence before expanding a list. Discovery fields and engagement ratios are incomplete evidence.
- [Keyword demand](https://treg.to/workflows/keyword-demand-to-ad-budget): discover terms, inspect live batch limits/prices and separate estimates from property observations. We did not adopt allocation by volume share.
- [Competitor creative](https://treg.to/workflows/mine-competitor-meta-ads-as-creative-pack): verify advertiser identity and inspect hooks, formats and destination. Active ads reveal neither profitability nor hidden targeting.
- [Customer research](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/customer-research): carry customer wording and counterexamples into decisions.
- [Marketing loops](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/marketing-loops): checks and actions can run on different conditions; state and recovery matter.
- [Google AI features](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) and [Apple product page optimisation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/): prefer first-party platform guidance to universal growth claims.

## Scope of this adoption

Seven focused skills were added: audience-research, customer-understanding, competitor-market-research, ai-visibility, creator-partnerships, conversion-retention and marketing-automation. The go-to-market placeholder became a coordinating draft; SEO review and content research were strengthened. The founder hub's obsolete placeholder statement and the customer-discovery boundary were updated.

AI visibility and creator partnerships are available for later briefs. No campaigns, creator outreach, research subscriptions, external skill installations or Hermes schedules were authorised by this packaging task. Project context, actual results and private data remain with each business.

The package is a working draft. Structural checks and author walkthroughs are recorded in LEARNING.md; they are not an independent evaluation or evidence of increased revenue.
