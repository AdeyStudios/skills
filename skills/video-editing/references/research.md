# Research and adoption

Reviewed 7 November 2026. This is a maintained decision record, not a list of current trending sounds or proof that the skill improves retention.

## Platform guidance

### TikTok: Creative best practices for performance ads

[Official guidance](https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/creative-best-practices?lang=en), updated June 2025.

TikTok recommends introducing an ad's proposition within three seconds and prioritising its hook in the first six. It also discusses vertical framing, safe zones, sound and testing substantially different creative ideas.

**Adopt:** early clarity, an attention-earning opening, phone framing and meaningful variation. **Qualify:** these are advertiser recommendations, not an instruction to wait six seconds before a short editorial pays off, nor evidence that an organic post needs a sales CTA, captions or a particular aesthetic. Specific timing choices still need playback and audience evidence.

### TikTok: Fashion 2.0 Creative Playbook

[Official playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/creativecenter/quicktok/online/Fashion_2024/pc/en), 1 April 2024.

The playbook presents creative observations rather than mandatory formulas and distinguishes formats by audience and objective. Its fashion examples span styling, product details, comparisons, transitions and graphic ideas.

**Adopt:** a visual premise and format appropriate to the brief; compare different reasons to watch. **Reject as blanket rules:** always use a logo opening, always use text, always look casual, or repeat an effect because it appears in a successful example. A platform's paid outcomes cannot be attributed to one isolated editing technique.

### TikTok: Fashion & Beauty Playbook 2026, AUNZ

[Official regional playbook](https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/AUNZ_TakeItToTikTok_Fashion_Beauty_Playbook.pdf), especially page 6 of the PDF.

The practical advice connects creative energy to audience, early attention, full-screen presentation, sound, and transitions that help viewers follow looks.

**Adopt:** match the energy to the viewer and keep the subject easy to follow. **Qualify:** this is regional commercial guidance; its promotional performance claims and general preference for casual footage do not establish the right finish or soundtrack for every fashion editorial. Silent delivery and deliberate polish can be explicit production requirements.

## Concrete fashion examples

### TikTok Shop: Art of the Edit — Fashion

[Official lesson and embedded examples](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?content_id=978423431333664&default_language=en-GB&learning_id=978423431382816), 26 July 2024.

The written lesson describes a TOMM Jewellery example pairing a point-of-view prompt with trying on an earring, and a Jan Collection example using a colour-choice prompt and changes of the same abaya in several colours. Its discussion connects the latter changes to the music and stresses clear product framing.

**Adopt:** attention can come from an understandable comparison or transformation; a cut changes something the viewer can identify. **Qualify:** a publisher's explanation is not independent measurement. Only describe exact timing or visual behaviour after inspecting the corresponding media, and do not claim to have heard beat alignment from a transcript or silent frame sheet.

**Media inspection during authoring:** downloaded the lesson's three public embedded videos into a temporary workspace. Timed frames from the jewellery example show the accessory and problem prompt immediately, application around three-to-five seconds, then the worn result. In the abaya example, frames show a question and a blue look immediately; detected colour changes occur at approximately 2.33, 5.23 and 7.50 seconds, with black, white and green looks. The stable composition makes each colour change legible. These are product demonstrations, not benchmarks for high-energy runway editorial. This was timed-frame inspection, not full-speed playback or audio listening; music synchronisation remains the publisher's account. The substantive lesson is identifiable change and an early premise, not copying these durations or their talking/posing sections.

### TikTok Newsroom: Loewe's runway presentation

[Official account of the campaign](https://newsroom.tiktok.com/loewe-fait-de-tiktok-son-tout-nouveau-podium?lang=fr), 9 March 2023.

TikTok documents a series of three-to-eight-second runway videos presenting looks through three camera views, including a platform-specific angle on accessories and shoes.

**Adopt:** concise fashion coverage can make meaningful perspective changes while retaining the clothes as its subject. **Qualify:** this is a historical campaign description, not direct playback analysis or a current trend report. Three views is not a requirement, and cropping a single view does not recreate separately filmed angles.

## Technique guidance

### CapCut: shaping a speed ramp

[Official workflow](https://www.capcut.com/features/how-to-speed-ramp-a-video-capcut-workflow), reviewed 7 November 2026.

Its guidance distinguishes varying speed within a shot from constant slow motion, centres retiming on the action, and calls for inspecting frame availability and interpolation.

**Adopt:** emphasise an event, compress less useful travel, and review the resulting motion. **Reject:** one ramp preset or identical timing across unrelated shots. This explains a technique, not its effect on retention; it does not require CapCut as the editor.

## What is interpretation rather than established evidence

The skill's recommendations to test first-frame interest, remove redundant visual information, vary rhythmic phrases and review a short hook before polishing are editorial working hypotheses informed by these sources and observed production failures. No universal cut frequency, ideal number of looks, guaranteed hook duration or engagement uplift is established here.

Reference access varies. During authoring, the public lesson text was accessible and embedded media could be discovered. Media inspection and its actual limits belong in the adoption record rather than being implied by a link. A blocked or expired video URL should lead to a disclosed limitation and an accessible reference, not an invented teardown. Keep temporary media out of this public repository.

## Updating the skill

When a reference or real edit reveals a useful lesson, record the source, the observed mechanism, the conditions, the smallest behaviour change and its check. Keep the principle only if it helps new work. If audience analytics are available, distinguish first-view creative judgement from early retention, completion and rewatch measurements; changes in length, distribution, audience and audio can confound a comparison.
