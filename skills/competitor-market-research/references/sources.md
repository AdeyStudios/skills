# Source decisions

Reviewed 15 November 2026.

- [Competitor profiling](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/competitor-profiling): dated evidence, comparable dimensions and explicit distinction between observation and interpretation.
- [PM Skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills): reference for competitive research and decision-oriented workflows. The collection is recorded for further study; its entire framework and maturity claims were not adopted.
- [Treg's competitor-ad workflow](https://treg.to/workflows/mine-competitor-meta-ads-as-creative-pack): inspecting an advertiser's actual identity, ads, hooks, formats and destinations. Treg's examples demonstrate retrieval, not competitor conversion performance.

We adapted these into a tool-independent workflow. Public ads do not expose private campaign economics; no missing field is filled with an invented traffic or spend estimate. A small comparative brief can be enough. Trial accounts, creative reuse and public claims remain separate permission and verification decisions.
