# Competitor market research

Working draft. Guidance has not yet demonstrated improved business outcomes.

Build a dated account of how alternatives reach and persuade a relevant audience, then identify experiments worth adapting.

## Research

Establish the user's product, audience and decision. Choose direct competitors and meaningful substitutes, including doing nothing or a manual workaround where relevant. Prefer a few justified comparisons to an exhaustive logo list. Inspect prior profiles before claiming something changed.

Use current official product pages, pricing, documentation, public listings and ad libraries, supplemented by dated reviews and public discussions. Verify advertiser identity and domain. Use available search/social/market data without assuming access or paying for a large export before sampling.

## Execute

- Compare the same dimensions: intended audience, promised outcome, supporting proof, offer, entry path, pricing basis, content/page types and visible channels. Retain currency, region, tax/trial context, billing period and observation date. Missing public pricing is unknown, not free.
- For ads, sample creative, hook, format, CTA, landing destination and observed start/status dates. Group recurring approaches into candidate messages. Ad presence or longevity does not establish profitability, spend, ROAS or the audience that converted.
- Inspect actual landing/product paths where public and authorised. A claim on a homepage is not proof a feature works. Record 'not observed on the pages checked' until a stronger absence claim is verified.
- Use reviews to identify questions and recurring themes; account for review selection and incentives. Keep observed fact, inference and proposed implication visibly separate. Never infer management's motives from a change alone.
- Recommend the smallest plausible adaptation, supported by an identified audience need and our product's real capabilities. Distinguish useful principles from copied creative or unsupported comparison claims. For an SEO-only task, focus on query/page patterns and verified source pages instead of expanding into every channel.

## Verify

Recheck claims that would change positioning, a purchase decision or a public comparison. Preserve original dates and source links; do not treat cached research as current. Report inaccessible journeys and unmeasured market metrics explicitly. End with what to test, what to preserve and what not to copy, with an observation window and a meaningful outcome.

Public research does not authorise trials using another person's identity, account creation, outreach, ad spending or publishing comparison pages. Follow the actual task's existing permissions.
