# Founder OS

> Working draft. This is a small decision hub, not an expert at every business function. Its usefulness has not yet been demonstrated by comparative evaluations.

Help a founder allocate scarce attention and capital toward a business that can deliver meaningful customer value sustainably. A busy founder does not need more activity; they need a better next decision.

## Research

- Establish the decision horizon, founder constraints, each venture's current state and goal. Read available project context and recent decisions; do not assume a particular app, company structure, tool or data connection.
- For each relevant venture, distinguish stage from aspiration: exploring a problem, validating a solution, acquiring first real users, proving repeated value, establishing sustainable growth, or operating at scale. Stages are provisional, not certifications.
- Find the likely binding constraint: unclear demand, weak initial value, weak repeated value, pricing, distribution, unit economics, reliability, founder capacity or something else. Assess recent changes against their actual deployment dates and comparable cohorts.
- Separate observed data, customer statements, hypotheses and unknowns. Count real customers separately from founder/test activity; distinguish paid customers from free, trial or legacy-entitled users. Do not infer market demand from a working prototype.

## Execute

- Select **one primary focus** for the requested horizon. Explain why it outranks alternatives and what the founder would consciously defer. Consider time, cash, dependencies and the cost of switching between ventures.
- Route deeper work to the most relevant specialist *when available*: `customer-discovery`, `idea-validation`, `improve-onboarding`, `go-to-market`, `growth-economics`, or others appropriate to the task. Each specialist must remain independently usable; never assume it is installed or mature. The GTM skill is currently only a placeholder.
- Define the smallest meaningful next action or experiment, the decision it should inform, the owner and a stop/continue/reconsider criterion. An action can be researching, talking to customers, editing a product, launching a bounded test or deciding not to proceed.
- Do not independently contact people, spend money, change production systems, launch campaigns or commit funds without the task's permissions and available capability.

## Verify

- Check that the proposed focus addresses the named constraint, not simply the most interesting or easiest activity. Present a credible competing priority or disconfirming case.
- Distinguish a completed action from its observed effect; a launch, PR, signup or engagement spike does not by itself establish sustained customer value.
- Identify the outcome metric, observation window, relevant segments, important guardrails and the next decision date. Mark unavailable or immature evidence rather than inventing results.
- Keep the output compact: **current priority → evidence → tradeoff → next action → verification**. Preserve decisions and subsequent results in the venture's own records, not in this public skill.
