# SEO content research

Find learning needs the product can answer well enough to deserve a visit. Choose content by evidence and usefulness, not equal coverage of product categories or an arbitrary publishing quota.

## Establish context

Use the supplied product, audience, existing pages, commercial goal and constraints. Inspect available project documentation and existing content before asking for information already present. Separate the research question from an assumed answer: an existing topic list is not evidence of search demand.

Use authorised first-party search and conversion data when available. Ask only for missing information that materially changes the work; continue research that does not depend on it.

## Investigate opportunities

- Group related searches by the underlying need and likely intent. Avoid a separate page for every keyword variant.
- Review current search results for the intended market and language. Record representative queries, sources and research date. Results are a sample that can vary by location and personalisation.
- Inspect the actual leading answers: accuracy, audience, clarity, examples, completeness and format. Explain the specific contribution our page could make. Do not assume established results are poor or easy to outrank.
- Use Search Console, keyword tools or other available evidence to assess demand. Search results, related questions and suggestions demonstrate wording or intent, not verified monthly volume. Do not invent volumes, difficulty scores or traffic forecasts. If demand is unmeasured, label it unmeasured.
- Check whether existing pages should be improved or consolidated before proposing new ones. Keep one page per distinct need, with related questions covered together.
- Connect each candidate to a plausible next useful product action without making every educational page a sales pitch.

## Make the recommendation concrete

For the strongest opportunities, provide the proposed title and page type, audience and intent, evidence, current answer gap, our useful contribution, product fit and uncertainty. Explain the ordering. A smaller defensible shortlist is better than padding weak recommendations.

For an educational article, outline a clear explanation, concrete worked example, important misconception and optional fresh application scenario. Give value before asking the reader to act. Visual subjects should use meaningful original or appropriately licensed illustrations when they improve understanding. Do not add a test or a comparison section merely to fill a template.

Keep proposed titles distinct from verified keywords. Cite primary sources for technical or factual claims, and source search evidence separately from the article's teaching material. If browsing or analytics is unavailable, produce a provisional hypothesis list and describe what needs checking.

## Measure and learn

Specify what would justify revisiting the recommendation: indexing, relevant impressions and clicks, useful engagement and downstream activation or conversion. Match measurements to the actual product; avoid promising rankings or interpreting new pages' short observation windows as failure.

Research does not itself authorise publishing pages, changing analytics or creating accounts. Implement content only within the user's requested scope.
