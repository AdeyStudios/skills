# Demand research decisions

Use when combining public audience signals, search analytics and paid keyword data.

Public conversation helps identify language, situations and possible unmet needs. Capture source and audience relevance, deduplicate, and preserve dissent. Search results help identify intent and the formats people are offered. Neither source yields verified monthly volume.

Search Console describes a particular property's observed exposure. Query-by-page dimensions are needed to connect a search to a URL; country/device filters and incomplete dates change the result. Keyword providers estimate a broader market using their own methods. Record their location, period and update date rather than merging unlike figures.

Before paid retrieval, inspect current capability, limits, coverage and pricing. Use authorised existing connections before adding a new dependency. Batch only compatible inputs and use a small coverage probe; a cheaper call is unhelpful when it returns the wrong geography or definition. Never import a website's quoted example prices as the current budget.

For each candidate, decide improve, consolidate, create, investigate, wait or reject. Attach the actual need, intent, matching pages, demand evidence, original contribution, product relevance, uncertainty and effort. A social trend, an estimated volume and an unfilled content calendar do not jointly prove a new article is worthwhile.
