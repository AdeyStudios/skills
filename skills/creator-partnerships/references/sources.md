# Source decisions

Reviewed 15 November 2026.

- [Creator discovery](https://treg.to/workflows/discover-creators-in-a-niche) and [creator screening](https://treg.to/workflows/screen-instagram-creators-before-outreach): discovery snapshots need current profile/post checks. Neither follower counts nor a simple engagement ratio establishes a suitable audience or profitable placement.
- [Influencer marketing](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/influencer-marketing): distinguish partnership goals, compensation, content deliverables and distribution. We use original generic guidance rather than adopting its volume programmes, numerical rate benchmarks or claims about platform suppression.

Treg's metered example runs are vendor demonstrations, not a budget estimate for a new brief. Obtain live prices and authorisation before paid retrieval. Actual audience data, fees, usage rights and conversion evidence may be unavailable and must remain unknown.

No creators were researched or contacted, no content rights granted and no campaigns created during this skill update. This skill can be kept for later use.
