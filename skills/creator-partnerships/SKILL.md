# Creator partnerships

Working draft. Guidance has not yet demonstrated improved business outcomes.

Match the audience and intended result to a suitable partner, then prepare a small, measurable test.

## Research

Identify the desired outcome: reach, credible introduction, useful content assets or attributable customer acquisition. These require different partner and deal choices. Use existing project context for audience, platform, market, budget and brand constraints. Treat follower bands and engagement thresholds as configurable screening decisions, not universal quality standards.

Use public discovery data and recent profiles/posts available through authorised tools. A discovery record is a dated snapshot. Sample before buying a large list; inspect live profiles and disclose unavailable audience demographics, reach or contact details.

## Execute

- Shortlist on audience relevance, content quality, posting activity, authentic discussion, fit with the product and the action the audience would take. Separate creator location from audience location. Likes divided by followers do not measure reach, genuine audience composition or purchase intent.
- Inspect a representative recent content sample, noting dates, paid posts, unusual engagement and missing/deleted content. Do not claim fraud from a suspicious ratio alone. Give a reason to keep, investigate or reject each material candidate.
- Compare partnership models appropriate to the goal: sponsored placement, commissioned creative, affiliate or longer relationship. Do not conflate paying for a creator's distribution with buying rights to use their footage in our ads.
- Prepare a concise brief with the audience's problem, credible promise, creative freedom, deliverables, destination and measurement. Identify the needed licence, reuse period, paid amplification, exclusivity and disclosure terms for review. Check applicable current platform and jurisdiction guidance when advising on those terms; the skill itself supplies no legal approval or media rights.
- Estimate scenarios from stated fees, production costs and plausible outcomes, labelling assumptions. Keep views, clicks, attributed purchases and incremental sales distinct. Do not quote a universal rate card or guarantee ROI.

## Verify

Verify identity, sources, current activity, available audience evidence and any public business contact before handing off. Define campaign tags or codes, attribution window and a quality/activation outcome alongside acquisition. Shared codes and last-click attribution have limits.

Research creates a shortlist or draft, not permission to send DMs, sign agreements, buy placements, scrape private sessions or use copyrighted content. Preserve existing explicit authorisations without repeatedly asking for them. No partnerships start just because the skill is installed.
