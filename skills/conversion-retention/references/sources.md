# Source decisions

Reviewed 15 November 2026.

- [CRO](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/cro) and [ASO](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/aso): inspect the actual entry surface and desired action; judge store visuals and copy in their intended context. We do not adopt universal layout prescriptions, scores or promised lifts.
- [Apple product page optimisation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/): first-party guidance on testing listing assets and inspecting outcomes. Its examples include a treatment that underperformed the original; prettier assets need not improve conversion.

Our synthesis distinguishes acquisition, first value, monetisation and repeated value. Measurement breaks and immature retention cohorts are alternative explanations to investigate before changing the product. Store capabilities, metadata limits and requirements must be checked against the current platform documentation when used.

The skill is an original working draft. No store experiment, customer message, price change or conversion improvement is claimed from its creation.
