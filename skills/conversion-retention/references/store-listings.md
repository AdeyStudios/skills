# Store listing mode

Use for App Store or Google Play acquisition work. Begin with the exact app, store, territory and locale. Inspect the live listing and current app/version where authorised; record what is public, what is available through the owner's console and what could not be inspected.

## Research

Separate search discovery, product-page conversion, installation, first useful action and payment. Store impressions, page views and downloads are different denominators. Keep source, device and locale consistent. Do not confuse App Store Connect with a public listing or assume edited assets are already live.

Read the actual icon, screenshots and preview. Check mobile readability, real product representation, order of customer benefits and consistency with what the installed version can deliver. Fetching HTML alone may not reveal image content. Store reviews are selected reports, not a census of users.

## Execute

Recommend a focused metadata or visual hypothesis tied to the intended audience. Verify current platform field limits, image/device sizes, supported experiment surfaces and review/release requirements before preparing upload-ready assets. Use meaningful product screenshots and authorised artwork; never invent screens, ratings or endorsements.

Preserve the existing baseline and distinguish uploaded, attached, submitted, approved and publicly visible states. Publishing or starting an experiment needs the task's actual permissions. When comparing assets, keep source and locale cohorts compatible and separate product changes occurring during the test.

## Verify

Inspect the final assets at listing/phone size and confirm the correct app and locale. Verify the live listing after publication when authorised; an upload success does not prove public display. Track installs alongside useful activation and later payment/retention where authorised. A more attractive treatment can underperform.

Primary guidance: [Apple product page optimisation](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/product-page-optimization/) and the [Google Play Console help centre](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/). Consult the current documents for the actual store task; do not transpose one store's metadata rules onto the other.
