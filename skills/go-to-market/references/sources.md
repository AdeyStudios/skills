# Source decisions

Reviewed 15 November 2026, after the user discussed the playbook and authorised a reusable skill set.

[Treg's GTM engineering playbook](https://treg.to/gtm-engineering) informed evidence-led qualification, price-aware retrieval, source tracking and staged automation. Its B2B examples and vendor-run studies are not universal targets for consumer products or services.

The linked [Marketing Skills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills), [PM Skills](https://github.com/phuryn/pm-skills) and [Swan GTM Skills](https://github.com/swan-gtm/gtm-skills) are reference collections. Their methods, host-specific commands and maturity claims were not imported wholesale. The product-context principle informed the project brief: context belongs with the business and may be supplied in any suitable format.

This coordinator is original synthesis alongside the existing founder, discovery, SEO, design and economics workflows. It selects proportionate work rather than creating a mandate to run every skill. Research, implementation, verification and business results remain distinct.

No campaign or recurring automation was launched. AI visibility and creator partnerships were added for future use. The research/operating drafts have not been proven to increase growth.
