# Project brief

Use when project context is missing or scattered. Reuse the project's existing format instead of demanding a new file. Keep one business's records separate from others.

Capture only what changes the current decision:

- Product, current useful capability and intended customer result.
- Audience, user/payer roles, market/language and meaningful exclusions.
- Current alternatives and why a real customer might switch.
- Offer, actual price/trial/access terms and relevant resource constraints.
- Evidence sources and dates: customer language, search behaviour, observed activation/return and economics. Mark hypotheses and missing data.
- Primary current constraint, previous experiments and work already in progress.
- Channels under consideration, what is authorised, and what is explicitly deferred.
- Proposed next action, baseline, outcome and counter-metric, owner and decision window.

Keep a short dated decision log: evidence changed, decision made, implementation state, observed result, next check. A merged change is not a growth result. Do not put credentials, customer identities or private metrics in the public skill collection.
