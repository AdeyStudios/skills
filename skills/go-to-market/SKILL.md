# Go to market

Working draft. Guidance has not yet demonstrated improved business outcomes.

Turn product and customer evidence into a focused route to useful customers. Keep this a compact coordinator; specialist workflows remain independent and optional.

## Research

Read the venture's existing audience, offer, product state, positioning, economics and recent experiments. Keep each business's context and records separate. Reuse known information and ask only for a missing answer that materially changes the decision.

Establish whether the current constraint is demand understanding, reaching suitable people, initial value, repeat value or monetisation. A distribution plan cannot compensate for a broken signup or absent repeated value. Fit an initial consumer product, a service and enterprise buying differently; B2B lead enrichment is an option, not the default GTM process.

Separate customer evidence, public discussion, provider estimates and business hypotheses. Record the evidence window and what is actually measured. For a new market, choose the cheapest decision-changing check before an elaborate funnel or automation stack.

## Execute

Choose a primary audience, the progress they want, a credible promise and one channel or experiment appropriate to the user's horizon. Where a broader plan is requested, show sequence, dependencies and which work is deferred.

Use focused methods when relevant and available:

| Decision | Helpful capability |
| --- | --- |
| What people discuss and how they describe the problem | Public audience research |
| What our actual customer evidence supports | Customer understanding or discovery |
| How alternatives position and distribute | Competitor market research |
| Which search needs deserve pages | SEO content research; technical review for discoverability defects |
| How we appear in AI answers | AI visibility research when requested or material |
| Who could introduce us to a relevant audience | Creator partnership research |
| Where visitors or customers lose value | Conversion, onboarding and retention diagnosis |
| Whether acquisition can pay back | Growth economics |
| Whether repeated work merits a schedule | Marketing automation design, separately authorised |

No named skill or connector is a required dependency. Do the needed bounded work with available evidence; disclose missing capabilities. Do not run the whole table for a narrow question.

Deliver an experiment brief: audience/problem, evidence, promise/offer, channel, proposed action, resource limit, outcome and guardrail, observation window and stop/continue decision. Allow a no-launch or no-change conclusion. Put shared business context and the decision log in that project's documentation, not in this public skill.

## Verify

Check that the action addresses the current constraint and the product can deliver the promise. Distinguish delivered work from customer impact. Track useful activation, repeat value or contribution revenue as appropriate, alongside channel counts. Preserve a baseline and conditions for a fair comparison; limited data should reduce confidence, not produce invented ROI.

The plan does not authorise ad spending, outreach, publication, account creation or recurring jobs. Complete only the work already authorised. See [source decisions](references/sources.md) and [the project brief](references/project-brief.md) when context needs organising.
