# Source decisions

Reviewed 15 November 2026.

[Customer research](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/customer-research) informed the distinction between analysing existing evidence, studying public discussion and commissioning new research. Its [product-marketing context](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/product-marketing) informed reusable, dated project context and customer language.

Adopted: trace findings to concrete records, preserve customer phrasing and use evidence to inform decisions. Our additional emphasis is on cohort eligibility, free-access bias, contradictory evidence, telemetry coverage and not inferring causation from a statement or replay.

The separate discovery skill remains useful for deciding what to ask or observe next. No repository, customer database or specific analytics product is required by this skill. The public hub holds the method; each project retains its authorised records. No customer research was conducted in creating this draft.
