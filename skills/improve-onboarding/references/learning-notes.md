# Onboarding learning notes

## Source and decision, 29 October 2026

These additions came from a user-supplied summary of an onboarding video and a discussion of the lessons to adopt. The original video URL, underlying benchmark study and experiment reports were not supplied or independently verified. The adopted instructions are design judgments to evaluate in context, not claims of established causal effects.

The agreed lessons were:

- Evaluate steps by their contribution to value, necessary setup or informed decisions, rather than imposing a screen-count target.
- Consider demonstrating the product and allowing a useful task before signup where appropriate.
- Make personalisation produce a visible benefit.
- Explain permissions when their purpose is relevant, with an outcome preview when useful.
- Prefer guidance tied to actual work; use checklists only when the task warrants them.
- Evaluate activation, retention and paid conversion together.

The summary named examples such as a lesson before account creation, personalised course recommendations, founder notes, task checklists and notification previews. These remain optional inspiration. We did not adopt the quoted percentage improvements, average flow lengths, broad cultural generalisations or a requirement for quizzes, animation, gamification or urgency.

If the original source becomes available, verify relevant details before citing its numbers. Add only lessons that change the workflow usefully; do not turn this note into a transcript archive.

## Behavioural design additions, 30 October 2026

Source: a user-supplied summary of "The psychology trick that makes any app feel 10x better". The video URL and benchmark data were not supplied, and the video itself was not reviewed. The user approved honest progress and meaningful success guidance after discussing its limits.

We adopted progress tied to actual remaining work, justified estimates and clean endings that explain the useful result and saved state. We rejected artificial processing delays, fabricated checks, automatic celebration and equating setup completion with learning. The video's "10x" framing and claimed progress-bar completion improvement remain unverified; these additions are design judgments to test in context. This skill contains the onboarding guidance it needs without requiring another skill to be installed.
