# AI visibility

Working draft. Guidance has not yet demonstrated improved business outcomes.

Separate observed visibility in real answers from content-readiness hypotheses.

## Research

Establish the audience, relevant buying or informational questions, market, language and target answer systems. Use existing project context and research rather than inventing a large prompt list. Check which systems, retrieval modes and reporting tools are actually available.

For an actual visibility audit, record the exact prompt, system/model when exposed, search mode, date and returned answer with source links. Use a bounded representative set that includes category, problem and comparison needs, not only brand-naming prompts. Do not simulate another service's answer using the current model and call that a measurement.

## Execute

- Distinguish a brand mention, recommendation and citation to an owned page. Inspect which third-party or competitor sources support the answer, and whether they are accurate and relevant.
- When the sample supports a rate, state its numerator, denominator, prompt set and repeated runs. A changing answer is expected; do not turn one run into market share or a deterministic rank.
- Inspect the actual cited or candidate pages: accessible content, clear explanations, reliable evidence, authorship where appropriate, entity consistency, and meaningful internal links. Check current official crawler and search guidance before proposing technical changes.
- If only page inspection is available, deliver a readiness assessment and label actual AI visibility unmeasured. A heuristic score is not proof of recommendation frequency, future citations or traffic.
- Prioritise concrete corrections and useful information gaps over formatting tricks. Do not mandate llms.txt, special schema or crawler access as a universal ranking requirement; distinguish documented eligibility from an untested tactic. A publisher may deliberately restrict a crawler.

## Verify

Preserve the baseline prompts and conditions for a comparable follow-up. Check implementation separately from subsequent answers and authorised referral/activation data. Record uncertainty, missing systems and how results may vary by geography or time. Never promise citations, fabricate mentions or pay for placements without authorisation.

Source links and limits are in [source decisions](references/sources.md). This skill can be installed for later use; its presence creates no monitoring schedule.
