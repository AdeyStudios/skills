# Marketing research additions

Reviewed 15 November 2026.

[Claude SEO](https://github.com/AgriciDaniel/claude-seo), linked from the [Treg playbook](https://treg.to/gtm-engineering), is a reference for considering technical, content and structured-data findings together. We retained the existing evidence-led audit and strengthened its handoff to demand research. We did not adopt the source's performance claims, automated scores or parallel-agent architecture.

[Google AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) supports keeping fundamental eligibility and useful content central. Actual AI-answer mentions require their own observations. An audit need not expand into every adjacent marketing channel.

The existing [site-audit procedure](site-audit.md) remains the comprehensive audit reference. Earlier audit findings still need fresh deployment and Search Console checks; research inspiration is not a reason to reopen resolved defects.
